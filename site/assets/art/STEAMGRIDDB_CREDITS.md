# SteamGridDB artwork credits

- `steamgriddb-hero.png` — [1920×620 alternate hero](https://www.steamgriddb.com/hero/86609) by Anon11926.
- `steamgriddb-logo.png` — [1416×815 official logo](https://www.steamgriddb.com/logo/54268) by Kam.
- `steamgriddb-icon.png` — [512×512 official icon](https://www.steamgriddb.com/icon/82211) by 8resolution.

`app-icon-master.png` is a generated Home Screen adaptation of the credited icon, with safe-area derivatives for iPad and installable web-app use. The source composition and colour identity remain based on 8resolution's SteamGridDB graphic.

The guide palette is derived from these assets: ink `#0c1114`, steel `#1a3f5c`, cyan `#3acdd2`, ice `#9ddce6`, Redux gold `#e0bf4c`, and violet `#60527e`.
